# Next mathematical attack

## Can ordinary outcome correlations certify that a learned update needs more states?

An episode contains independent fair input signs followed by an ordinary binary outcome. An unknown homogeneous $k$-state stochastic update reads the signs in order. The terminal head is linear in the state probability, as for a mixture of statewise output distributions. For each subset of input positions, its signed product times the observed outcome estimates a Fourier coefficient of the full prediction table without observing a state. If a coefficient always vanishes in a combination across different position masks, a measured violation could refute a physical state-count claim.

A two-state selector calculation gave one antisymmetry: coefficients at the first and second positions and at the first and third positions agree. Investigate the general source. After one selected input, the row of signed state masses has total mass zero. Does this make all universal homogeneous linear identities on subsequent masks exactly the two-generator polynomial identities of ordinary $(k-1)$-dimensional matrices? Check the reverse construction with strictly positive stochastic transitions, reset probabilities and outcome rows. Treat identities involving other initial-position layouts or nonlinear decoders as distinct questions.

**Numerical audit registered before running it.** For the boundary between three and four states, compare all words of lengths zero through five in two arbitrary $2\times2$ integer matrices by independent modular elimination. Check the predicted nullities $0,0,0,0,0,2$ at those lengths, two proposed fifth-degree commutator identities, and a concrete strictly positive four-state realization violating an identity after six signs. Enumerate its ordinary raw records and compare their Fourier coefficients to matrix products in exact arithmetic. For the general upper witness, evaluate an alternating product of successive powers of one matrix separated by the other and test a diagonal matrix against a one-step shift for dimensions two through five. Count operations and the cost of estimating a signed identity from independent records. Investigate what this can certify about computational state and what it leaves unresolved about acquiring the learner itself.
