# Next mathematical attack

Does the exact hard-address regime survive a change from binary prediction registers to finite probability grids, and what survives a continuous limit?

For $m$ equally spaced levels consider an affine map of register values. Count the input grid points on which a genuinely multivariate update lands on the same grid. Compare exact execution to computing a continuous convex mixture then rounding it. The two need not have the same physical or predictive behavior under long composition. This test connects the finite-grid next-token learner to the earlier signed-density theorem; it does not presume that natural text fills a cube of predictive states.

The broader uncertainty is learning a useful low-dimensional observation basis when passive data cover only a sparse part of the full vocabulary and its continuations. The priced-search certificate starts after a basis has been fitted. A prospective claim of physical advantage must include that acquisition and evaluation on a strong, comparably trained alternative.
