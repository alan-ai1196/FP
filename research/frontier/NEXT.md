# Next mathematical attack

## When do ordinary word frequencies identify the hidden update?

Arbitrary single-tape likelihood optimization with a one-bit state can encode Max-Cut. On two natural word blocks a fitted general table used feedback, yet a learned two-bit suffix did better. Realizability gives a different question. Suppose a stationary source assigns every word positive probability in both states, their emission rows differ, and each observed word deterministically updates the state. Test whether the direction of the next-word distinction after one more raw name reveals its action, which order of word frequencies is necessary, and what observable quantity measures the time needed to infer an unknown starting state. [Agnostic lower bound](FOUNDATION.md#learning-a-one-bit-update-can-encode-a-maximum-cut-on-one-token-stream) · [Natural word comparison](EVIDENCE.md#learning-which-raw-words-preserve-or-erase-a-bit)

### Registered finite positive-source test

Use three autonomous four-symbol sources. The first has actions (write-0, write-1, hold, flip) with rows (6,2,5,3)/16 and (2,6,3,5)/16. The second uses (write-0, write-0, hold, flip) with the same emission rows. The third uses (hold, hold, flip, flip) and rows (6,2,4,4)/16 and (2,6,4,4)/16. Shuffle raw names for each of six seeded repeats per source. Draw a stationary training path, and independent stationary validation and reporting paths of 8,192 and 16,384 words. Fit from nested training prefixes of 4,096, 32,768 and 131,072 words. Simulation alone has access to the hidden state and the true table.

Choose the largest absolute empirical adjacent-pair covariance as the prefix/outcome witness. For each middle name, compare the witness outcome frequency after that name when the earlier word is or is not the witness prefix. Use positive/negative changes relative to the pair distinction to propose hold/flip. Try zero thresholds of 0.5, 2, 4 and 8 pooled standard errors and every single split of the putative reset words ordered by their one-word conditional outcome frequency, including a common reset destination. These are candidate generators; the standard errors do not certify confidence on an overlapping tape. Fit heads from ordinary training tokens with a half-count per output and positive Q16 quantization. Select by ordinary validation likelihood, integrating equally over the two possible initial bits on each fresh sequence. Compare against the same fitter and selector over all 256 tables, and direct constant, bigram and trigram controls. Report action recovery up to state relabeling, report loss, candidate work and failures at every prefix size. Keep the natural words disjoint from this realizability check.
