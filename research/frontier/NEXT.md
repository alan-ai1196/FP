## Does the history rule still help after fitting a stronger static predictor?

A learned last-word correction helped the original lexical row and hurt a more strongly smoothed row on both earlier windows. Refit the rule itself after optimizing its background. Use the same past 524,288 background and 262,144 lexical-fitting tokens starting at 9,000,000, the same 50,257 outputs and the same eleven frequent names plus remainder. Split the following 65,536 chronological validation tokens in half. Use the first half for base selection and the second for history-rule selection. Neither reporting window enters either choice.

**Static base competition.** Compare the previous additively smoothed trigram with interpolated absolute-discount Kneser–Ney trigrams having shared discounts $1/4,1/2,3/4,9/10$. Use continuation counts at the lower orders, a half-count per output in the continuation unigram and full backoff for unseen contexts. Fit all five trigrams on the same background. For each, compare lexical mixture weights $1/16,1/8,1/4,1/2,3/4,1$ plus the pure trigram. Smooth the lexical distribution toward the background raw unigram with $64,1024,8192,65536,262144,1048576$ pseudocounts or use the prior exactly. Fit by twelve EM updates as before. Also consider the previously verified static average of two word-specific-switching lexical rows with its original trigram. Choose the lowest first-half validation NLL. Score this base alone on the reports.

**Fitting the history again.** On the chosen base fit the registered rolling two-state, last-two, free-table, product, shifted-history and independent discrete-row rules using all 462 candidate word partitions and the earlier strength grid. Keep the earlier bit trained on the old pooled row as a transplant control. Add a separately optimized comparator allowing the eight tilts to vary continuously in $[-3/4,3/4]$. For each partition solve its eight one-dimensional concave objectives in the coordinate $\eta=u/(1+u m)$ by at least 24 bisection steps and choose the largest training gain. Permit global attenuation of $\eta$ by $0,1/2,1$ on the second validation half. If the winning static choice has zero lexical weight, the registered corrections to that component are identically zero. Report that outcome. A shared component with weight zero can still be tested on the best positive-weight base as a separately labeled diagnostic. Compare all models against the selected static base, the earlier weaker base and the selected pure trigram. Record per-source totals, eight blocks, parameters and full-row normalization. No mathematical claim about every model class is inferred from a fitted zero.

Score 65,536 targets beginning at index 23,000,000 of the training source and 1,710,000 of the reporting source. The SHA-256 digests of the two prefixes through the window ends are 09e677b7d238c0be7e5b9c2baed808270adc31eb9cebd0ea91c142b554dd6daa and 9122dd93250446a4d95c637bf34283c21ba64aae4be3df8af17b55c82b6bce8e.
