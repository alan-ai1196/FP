# Next mathematical attack

How much precision does a history distinction need, and does spending those bytes improve ordinary prediction compared with spending them on raw context rows? The full-vocabulary test learned 1,019 changes to the frequency address. It beat its seed after storage cost. An equally sized trigram with fitted output probabilities beat the learned map in a later diagnostic on the same reporting block. For two fixed positive readouts, the conditional log-likelihood ratio gives an exact objective for binary assignments. The next question is whether an address that can install an intermediate mixture of the readouts justifies both its extra precision and its execution cost.
