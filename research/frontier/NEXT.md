# Next mathematical attack

The rare-answer result establishes which updates an optimal causal machine must implement when the input action is supplied. Its noisy learner can infer the hidden dimension and directions, yet raw toggles still supply a geometry. ## Can ordinary word continuations identify the input action itself?

Give a learner two uninterpreted raw letters. Determine when observable responses imply that their unknown predictive transitions commute or square to the identity, what a finite horizon certifies under a state bound, and how many raw queries can be needed to rule out an unobserved violation. Fit a learner that is permitted arbitrary transition tables and compare it with a learner that assumes a toggle law.

## Frozen registrations

### Inferring token updates without a supplied algebra, 2026-11-26

Fresh seeds 96–111, two raw letters A and B, one query marker and ordinary binary answers. Each source has four hidden reachable states, starts at zero and has one favored response state. Use random choices from NumPy [seed,kind,8511]. For kind 0 choose two distinct nonzero two-bit increments uniformly without replacement for the two letters and let them act by XOR. For kind 1 use the cycle 0→1→2→3→0 and its inverse. For kind 2 use that four-cycle and the transposition exchanging states 0 and 1. In kinds 1 and 2 permute the choice of which letter gets which map with a fair coin. Choose the favored state uniformly after choosing the maps. Ordinary response probability is 7/8 in the favored state and 1/8 elsewhere.

Use every word of length zero through seven in length then lexicographic order. At each of 33 repetitions use a uniform permutation of the 255 words from [seed,kind,8512], emit their letters followed by query and ordinary target, and generate each answer from that same stream of independent random numbers. Fit at cumulative 1, 3, 9 and 33 repetitions. This is exhaustive short-word exposure; price all words, tokens and repetitions. No learner sees hidden states, transition labels, the true number of states or the algebra. The general family permits any accessible, minimal deterministic one-to-four-state Moore machine, with each raw letter an arbitrary transition and binary readout. Enumerate once using canonical breadth-first state naming. Use equal size prior on one through four and uniform conditional prior on distinct minimal rooted machines. Compute exact posterior predictive probabilities and the highest-posterior individual machine. The other family permits all 16 binary functions of the two raw letter-count parities with uniform prior. Compare both and their equal-prior evidence mixture. The noise rate is shared.

At each fit evaluate ordinary pre-answer probabilities against the true conditional law on 256 new words. Use [8513] to generate 128 uniform 24-letter words followed by 128 uniform 25-letter words, shared across sources and never used in fitting. Use a positive Q16 floor across the five raw symbols, the same known word-length protocol for all heads, and the true marginal of the evaluation panel as baseline. Report full-stream and query gain, exact behavior of the installed machine by product-state traversal, classification of its two token laws, and forward-transition storage. Exhaust the small machine catalogue to verify the finite witness horizon. Recover the induced action from the noiseless short-word signs independently as a check. Distinguish an exact conclusion with a justified state bound from any generalization beyond it.
