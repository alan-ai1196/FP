## Can ordinary observations certify transfer when the channels are unknown?

Training sometimes pairs an old view with the desired output and sometimes pairs the old and new views without an output. The intended prediction uses the new view. Even for a fair binary latent state, old-view, new-view and target amplitudes $(1/2,1/2,1/2)$ or $(3/4,1/3,1/3)$ give the same two observed joint laws, and new-view/target correlations $1/4$ and $1/9$. An independent twin old read measures its squared amplitude and distinguishes these two worlds. Determine which additional ordinary pairings certify a transferable full output law for multiple latent states and categorical reports. How much does weak calibration cost in independent episodes, total reports and retained state? A spectral projection can miss directions common to the target and the new view. Conditional report probabilities are also nonnegative and normalized on each latent state. Establish whether these restrictions change the attainable transfer or the best robust prediction, and whether a feasible calibration query improves it. The text result remains an empirical check on any claim that separate files share the requisite task.
