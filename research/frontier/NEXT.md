# Next mathematical attack

## Can ordinary loss construct an entire product when every proper subproduct is uninformative?

Given a residual array $R_{i_1,\ldots,i_L,j}$ from a normalized next-token predictor, search jointly for previously absent raw-name tables $w_1,\ldots,w_L$ and output signs $s$, with logit shift $\beta s_j\prod_\ell w_\ell(i_\ell)$. Investigate a direct finite-data algorithm. Contract the first axis by signing its fiber vectors so their squared accumulated norm never falls below the sum of their squared norms. Repeat on the smaller array and choose the sign of the final output vector. Determine the guaranteed training gain, arithmetic and storage in terms of observed cells, the ability to recover planted factorizations, and the reach of iterative growth for a finite full conditional table. Identify the distinction between a useful empirical direction and learning a factorization on unseen tuples.

**Prospective ordinary-label acquisition test.** Draw an independent balanced unknown sign assignment for each of $L=2,3,4$ slots with eight raw input names each. The next raw label is binary with conditional mean $\epsilon\prod_\ell w_\ell(i_\ell)$, for $\epsilon=0,1/2,3/4$. The learner receives only raw inputs and their next label. On full uniform inputs, all proper subsets of the slots carry exactly zero predictive signal. Hold out a random fourth of full tuples before collecting training labels and report the exact population loss on the masked tuples. Use 16 independent assignments and masks per length, with nested raw training sizes 256, 2,048 and 16,384. The nonzero strengths can be supplied equally to sign-table comparators. A training or separate calibration option that can deploy a fair prediction is necessary on the null. Record sample use, execution costs and all hidden-table recovery criteria up to harmless sign changes.

**Comparisons.** Compare deterministic fiber signing, conventional leading singular vectors of the mode unfoldings, an alternating fit from those starts, direct smoothed full-tuple counts, and ordinary lower-order fits. Include a comparison to an exhaustive optimum on genuinely small binary-name cases, and audit the norm guarantee independently with exact integer arrays. Show whether the new constructor can form a predictive computation absent every useful one-input predecessor, and whether the stronger conventional factor estimator does as well. Ordinary language and finite-resource interaction remain separate tests.
