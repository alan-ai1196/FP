## Objective and literature-driven comparisons

Use separate predictive, emergence and interactive claims, each with a budget and a specified observer. Retain the finite-state learner and the order-two counterexample as controls. A bound on conditional loss under a logging policy transfers to decisions only with action coverage. A later task can also change what state was worth constructing before it was known. The seven-bit parity theorem supplies an exact optimum against arbitrary finite encoders. Its Hamming construction is a comparison target for learning. ## What ordinary labels identified in the seven-bit test

Empirical likelihood recovered an optimal stored computation and all 128 decoders from ordinary noisy examples. The permitted operations were XORs; the objective chose their shared masks from all four-dimensional layouts. Exact prediction required a joint choice. The greedy learner learned 112 of the task rules even though each step optimized immediate likelihood within its available choices. Keep exhaustive search as a check on efficacy and the unrestricted raw-parity reader as a check on total cost. A single-use query favors the latter in the simple XOR/tap schedule. [Registered results and amortization thresholds](EVIDENCE.md#learning-a-four-bit-synopsis-from-noisy-tokens)

## Which part of a composition was learned?

The likelihood and storage price selected one task-coordinate product on a structured world and generalized to every held-out task. A two-byte operation reconstructed its composition on all ordered task pairs. Held-out likelihood selected posterior lookup under random naming. The recovered operation acts on task descriptions while ordinary raw-response XOR defines what constitutes a correct composition. Treat learning the raw-response operation itself as the next distinct claim. An exact Foundation R4 realization of the earlier synopsis also shows that its access pattern and its algebraic execution are compatible with the existing native language. ## When did the learner identify its response operation?

The latest predictor learned the binary operation itself from ordinary label likelihood and used that learned table on fresh inputs with a finite adaptation budget. A matched Bayesian learner reproduced its scores. The binary fold grammar, access to deliberately picked pilot inputs, and storage for the opaque dictionary were essential assumptions. Removing input selection makes full-dictionary acquisition exponentially expensive for one of the two equally small operations. Changing to a three-state accumulator can preserve every registered calibration answer and change future forecasts. Treat acquisition cost under naturally occurring observations as the constraint that now most affects transfer to ordinary streams. [Evidence and hardware accounting](EVIDENCE.md#learning-the-response-operation-from-opaque-task-names) · [Passive observation bounds](FOUNDATION.md#the-cost-of-waiting-for-informative-inputs)

## Did ordinary text pay for a learned operation on the frequency bit?

The winning truth table complemented the previous bit and reversed its decoder rows. It improved on a calibrated bigram, tied the original lag, and lost to a similarly priced raw trigram. Close this one-bit operation search. The subsequent question is which raw tokens can share a computational action, since changing the recurrence over a fixed partition can discard a distinction before learning begins. [Prospective test](EVIDENCE.md#selecting-a-binary-memory-update-on-ordinary-text)

## Which raw tokens may execute the same action?

Retain normalized two-sided context probabilities as an exact criterion for lossless internal symbols with a fixed within-cell refinement. The sharp observation horizon is $2d-1$ for a known finite predictive rank $d$. Ordinary three-token likelihood learned the two genuine alias pairs on the six-token source; pair likelihood erased a distinction that costs 0.053465 nats per token on an unlimited stationary stream. A raw-context-dependent within-cell decoder changes the mathematical question. The exact criterion and data leave acquisition of a physically affordable general causal update open.
