## What ordinary labels identified in the seven-bit test

Empirical likelihood recovered an optimal stored computation and all 128 decoders from ordinary noisy examples. The permitted operations were XORs; the objective chose their shared masks from all four-dimensional layouts. Exact prediction required a joint choice. The greedy learner learned 112 of the task rules even though each step optimized immediate likelihood within its available choices. Keep exhaustive search as a check on efficacy and the unrestricted raw-parity reader as a check on total cost. A single-use query favors the latter in the simple XOR/tap schedule. ## Can a short-window learner install an update for much longer histories?

Within the positive, two-state permutation class it can. All observable transition signs and emission rows are identifiable from one-letter statistics and a single selected slice of three-token windows. The registered learner used a full histogram and learned a signed addition in log odds, reaching 1.677701 nats per token on 64-position episodes. A conventional two-state Bayesian filter made the same predictions. The proof and experiment justify a shared categorical and continuous representation for this class. Its hidden-state and positive-permutation assumptions must be tested before transferring it to a richer source. [Evidence](EVIDENCE.md#training-on-three-tokens-and-forecasting-sixty-four)
