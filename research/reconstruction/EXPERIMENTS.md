# Decisive tests of priced prediction

Predictions recorded before executing the reconstruction audits. Finite toy tests concern individual mathematical claims. The language comparison concerns the original full-alphabet text distribution.

## Exact tests on finite experiments

* Present the same fair binary prediction experiment in direct and invertibly parity-coded cells, for widths 2 through 5, both clean and with noise $1/4$. Enumerate partial transcripts. Any unseen required cell must leave the label exactly fair. Mixing full access and zero access must attain the expected-read bound.
* Give one uniformly hidden persistent source among $M$ independent fair columns. Train by forecasting the next binary label then observing it. For $M=2,3,4$ and zero through three fully observed ordinary training rows, enumerate hidden worlds and rows. After $n$ labels, the surviving count is distributed as $1+\mathrm{Binomial}(M-1,2^{-n})$. Among machines delivering the full Bayes forecast on every training row, filtering surviving columns consumes exactly $n+2(M-1)(1-2^{-n})$ expected feature reads. Check the risk for every fresh read budget from zero through $M$, including intermediate training forecasts.
* Independently optimize every next read, including adaptive choices and early stopping, for small unequal posteriors on independent columns. Under hard $k$ equal-price reads the $k$ largest posterior masses attain minimum log loss. With positive noise all positive-mass columns remain necessary for an exact full forecast. Correlating two columns should break the ranking. Compare also with an unrestricted conditional table and ordinary Bayes matching on the identical observations.
* Give a deployment encoder knowledge of the true uniform hidden index, at most $K$ messages and one fresh bit read. Enumerate the partitions and decoder choices through $M=7$. For informative noise the optimum cell sizes are $K-1$ singletons and one cell of size $M-K+1$. This is an oracle upper bound on the performance of a finite-data learner. Check a corresponding reachable finite-data code separately with an independent hidden-world enumeration. Compare optimal log risks by exact likelihood products or exact log identities.
* Verify conditional probability under a binary symmetric channel by summing over planted worlds with ordinary labels, then compare the sign-dot-product likelihood odds. A reversed sequence must recover equal weights from exact sufficient counts even when a direct binary16 posterior had rounded a weight to zero. Test simultaneous relabeling and stochastic postprocessing of distributions. Check the independent-code fixed point and a pair interaction outside the readout family.

### Additional temporal prediction, registered before its check

If every time coordinate permits independent downstream stochastic postprocessing, the tagged joint output should be a law of address paths. Independently enumerate two-address experiments through length four, including known positive noise. In particular, uniform even and odd address-parity laws should have observational total variation $2^{-d}(1-2\eta)^d$ per $d$-event block. With clean labels, optimal testing error after $n$ blocks should be $(1-2^{-d})^n/2$. Verify passive reconstruction of an asymmetric rational path law from label/column correlations, the parity distributions on every revealed history, and the mass of the distinguishing event. These predictions concern reset blocks with independently fair source bits.

### Direct comparison against the original positive algebra

The earlier interaction falsifier can be tested against the actual R4 exact evaluator. Use two binary inputs, four equiprobable contexts, and a fresh label given by XOR with independent flip probability $1/4$. In training cycle through the four contexts four times, flipping the last label for each context. This is a designed count fixture. Forecast before observing each outcome. A conventional add-one table and an R4 positive delayed program computing the same observed pair counts should agree before every label and both give probability $2/3$ to the clean label after training. Neither initializer has the XOR assignment. In the opaque routing arm even let an oracle choose any mixture of the two noisy source values at each context. At input $(1,1)$ unanimity forces probability $3/4$ on label 1, for which the actual probability is $1/4$. Predict that the two trained table realizations strictly beat even this oracle router in expected log loss. This tests semantic reach with an explicit causal update and supplied computation. General graph discovery and hardware timing are distinct questions.

## Prospective ordinary-token experiment

The first empirical question is whether the routing premise describes any appreciable part of next-token loss. All arms receive the same causal token prefix and score the full vocabulary. For a bounded test of **transfer**, use a fixed positive predictive distribution for each past-token cell (copy with a smoothed unigram escape), plus a base-only cell. Learn a *persistent* source from ordinary labels by the likelihood update. The read values depend on earlier tokens. Their correlations and reuse violate the fair-independent-column theorem. The predicted failure mode is concentration on a single globally useful lag while the held-out text needs different lags and label-changing features in different contexts.

Use the repository's 1,048,576 training tokens, tuning prefix [0,4096) and development suffix [4096,16384), in the original causal order and with the same boundary rules. A protocol would fix lag range 1–512, escape probability, positive frequency smoothing, reset policy, numerical error allowance, and an out-of-sample decision rule before opening results. Use identical smoothing and initial information in the conventional dense Bayesian mixture and a Foundation positive realization. On identical histories the cleanly implemented dense mixture must agree with the proposed learner. Give the implementation its best indexed lookup and shared denominators. A claimed advantage over that predictor *in prediction* would refute the implementation's interpretation. A claimed saving in deployment reads requires including the cost of updating, ordering and retaining the weights, data movement, entropy output, and any cost of acquiring side information. Record peak bytes, training/inference wall time, update work, token exposures and loss separately.

Compare also against the historical modified Kneser–Ney and Transformer anchors on the same view. For a competitiveness claim, additionally train a well-tuned causal Transformer with efficient device attention on the same token exposure, selection budget and measured train/inference envelopes, alongside its best unconstrained run on the same data. Expose any unmatched axes. A naive dense attention implementation is an invalid speed baseline. Tiling changes the cost of an exact attention computation on hardware. [Original baseline protocol](../../experiments/next_token/BASELINE_ANCHOR_A1.md) · [Dao et al., FlashAttention](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2022/hash/67d57c32e20fd0a7a302cb81d36e40d5-Abstract-Conference.html)

One inexpensive **exact exclusion before training** is the probability assigned to labels absent from every available copy cell. The report derives the bound. If its summed contribution already exceeds a comparator's full loss under a common protocol, that copy-only class cannot win. A low bound is inconclusive. The decisive positive result would be learning when different observations deserve work on real text, with lower matched cost at equal loss or better loss at equal cost than the strongest matched alternative. Persistent-source semantics currently provide no context-dependent likelihood law that would warrant a large GPU training run. Any later device claim needs a verified mixed-precision path and complete matched resources on the single 24-GB RTX 3090.

### Fixed-class text capacity test selected for execution

Test the stronger frozen class of *all* mixtures of 513 source distributions. Source 0 is the normalized add-one unigram from the original training prefix (the published anchor's selected smoothing). The other 512 distributions put unit mass on the token at their respective causal lag. Every fixed smoothed-copy mixture with this base is included. Weights are arbitrary, nonnegative, sum to one, and are shared across every scored position. Every original lag is available at the start of the development suffix. The fixed-class claim includes parameters arbitrarily close to a simplex face and weights fitted with knowledge of every scored label. This makes the lower bound applicable to any learning algorithm deploying one such fixed vector.

For token $y_t$, let $a_{tj}$ be its probability under source $j$. For any rational interior-base witness $u$, let $q_t=\sum_j u_j a_{tj}$ and $s_j=N^{-1}\sum_t a_{tj}/q_t$. Jensen's inequality gives a bracket for the hindsight minimum over the whole simplex:

$$-\mathrm{mean}\log q_t-\log\max_j s_j\ \le\ \inf_w L(w)\ \le\ -\mathrm{mean}\log q_t.$$

Seek the witness by ordinary mixture EM on the **development suffix** for the sole purpose of tightening this mathematical certificate. Use 128 float64 sweeps from initial mass $1/2$ on the base and $1/1024$ per lag. Floor lag weights to multiples of $2^{-24}$ and assign the remainder to the base. From that point compute with integer masses, round the score ratios upwards and enclose the logarithms. Verify the actual prefix identities against the completed anchor. Use the original 12,288 scored tokens without subsampling. Also give the pointwise support lower bound for an arbitrary context-dependent mixer of the *same* sources. Predict that the fixed-class lower exceeds the recorded Transformer suffix score. Comparison with order-5 MKN is a separate two-sided diagnostic. If the bracket overlaps a score, record an inconclusive comparison. No fitted witness serves as a deployable model or a trained test score. The presence of a strict gap would settle this candidate's fixed-source language premise without a training or GPU experiment. The small audits and this one finite text decision suffice for the current investigation.
