## Strong baseline, measurements and interpretation

The comparator maintains all 2^n latent assignments, including global flips.
For ordinary IID and the selected stress tape it uses initial weights
`9^(matching training labels)`; for the two conditioned diagnostics reuse
the previously executed conditioned posterior. Before every target, compute
the noisy parity forecast from all current weights, capture actual half
excess / single base / normalization words, then multiply each weight by 9
when it matches that revealed label. This keeps cycles and all later labels.
The baseline's actual tensor allocation remains bounded by 16/32 MiB; its
own integer payload and assignment visits are reported, without pretending
its value constructor is available to FP as a fitted initializer.

Retain full-domain and unseen-subset expected CE/Brier, raw single-division
CE, calibration and latent relation error. The unseen subset excludes the
initial undirected training edges and diagonals throughout; it is a fixed
subset, not a claim that later queries contain no transitive information.
Keep RN-4's exact per-query Brier and fixed 48-bit interval averaging, avoiding
a huge cross-query denominator. Independently replay complete CUDA and
binary64 phase histories in each sealed worker, then independently reconstruct
forecasts and fresh installation decisions in post-analysis. No model or GPU
execution occurs in that post-analysis, and failed tails receive no scores.

V5 still fixes empirical within-component signs. A wrong within-component
parity cannot be corrected by its nonnegative scale; its true preferred
probability is at most 1/2 at every learner state. This is an expressivity
limitation of the emitted family, not erasure by Runtime. The later analysis
must distinguish it from inadequate data, optimization, range and precision.
Cross-query symmetries at one fixed state must not be mistaken for a lower
bound on scores collected at different evolving states.

Known-case comparisons change constructor, learning rate, range, resource
allowance and evidence bound together. They do not isolate one causal effect.
No fixed tape establishes a population rate, stochastic premise, Bayes
dominance or restarted-family guarantee. Retain compact source-bound outcomes,
completed jobs and independent checks; omit weights, datasets and bulk phase
histories. Do not retune a failed case, add a winning rate, enlarge its budget
or reopen static resource theory in response to its score.
