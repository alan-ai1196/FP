## Question and claimed result

For an elliptic curve $E/\mathbb Q$, define

\[
s_p(E)=\operatorname{corank}_{\mathbb Z_p}\operatorname{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E/\mathbb Q).
\]

This is the full Selmer group with local Kummer conditions at every place. Its corank can include a divisible contribution from the Tate–Shafarevich group. Replacing it with the Mordell–Weil rank would change the hypothesis.

Theorem 1.1 asserts that, for every prime $p$, if $s_p(E)=r\in\{0,1\}$, then

\[
\operatorname{ord}_{s=1}L(E,s)=\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Z}E(\mathbb Q)=r,
\qquad \#\Sha(E/\mathbb Q)<\infty.
\]

The conclusion concerns the entire Tate–Shafarevich group. The stated theorem imposes no ordinary-reduction or residual-irreducibility restriction on the original curve. It does not calculate the exact BSD leading coefficient, which is the subject of companion manuscripts including OAI-1006-002 and OAI-1006-004.

The paper is theoretical. It reports no numerical experiment, trained model, benchmark or computational test of the general assertion. Its application in §10 concerns rational cube sums.
