## Original problem and claimed result

For a rational elliptic curve E and a prime q, the manuscript assumes that the full q-primary Selmer group has Z_q-corank zero or one. It claims equality of that corank, the Mordell–Weil rank and the analytic rank, finiteness of the entire Tate–Shafarevich group, and the full Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer leading-term formula. The hypothesis is about Selmer corank. Mordell–Weil rank at most one alone is not the stated hypothesis.

The claimed formula is

\[
\frac{L^{(r)}(E,1)}{r!}
 = \frac{\Omega_E\,\operatorname{Reg}_E\,\#\Sha(E/\mathbb Q)
                \prod_\ell c_\ell(E)}{\#E(\mathbb Q)_{\rm tors}^{,2}}.
\]

The L-function is uncompleted and retains all finite Euler factors. The period is the total integral over E(R), including both components when present. The regulator uses a generator basis of the full free Mordell–Weil lattice and the manuscript's height H. Replacing that lattice by an index-d sublattice multiplies its regulator by d squared. These conventions matter to the exact formula.

There are no numerical experiments or benchmark comparisons. The evidence offered is a mathematical argument with substantial imported arithmetic and automorphic inputs.

## Argument through the ten sections

### 1–2. Arithmetic normalization and auxiliary fields

The Kummer exact sequence turns whole-Sha finiteness into equality of all p-Selmer coranks with the Mordell–Weil rank. This is how the initial q-primary assumption eventually permits the two-primary input.

The auxiliary negative discriminants D and D′ are chosen with prescribed splitting, coprimality, residual disjointness and ranks of three twists. The density argument first fixes D and then uses two fixed curves. It does not require a uniform density estimate over an unbounded family of curves. The resulting real quadratic F and CM biquadratic L support the later theta construction.

The Heegner-point identity is an equality of arithmetic ratios before taking a p-adic valuation. Its index is the full index [E(K):Z P_K], including torsion. The Gross–Zagier normalization retains the Manin constant, discriminant period factor and degree-two comparison of heights. The later central determinant must reproduce these factors exactly.

### 3–4. Integral finite models and added tame directions

Section 3 contracts residual cochain complexes and transfers differentials, maps, products and homotopies to bounded finite free models. Uniform residual dimension and cohomological range bounds are hypotheses. Compatible finite-precision limits use a nonprincipal ultrafilter. This gives existence of models, without an effective construction or quantitative complexity bound.

Local complements are established over the testing ring, with their degree-zero terms and cup nullhomotopies. Fraction-field orthogonality alone would not suffice. Line and square switches have specified amplitudes, pairings and primitive reductions. The square switch uses a class-functional dual, rather than treating a rational complementary basis as automatically integral.

The bounded-series division lemma assumes bounded numerators and denominators and a nonzero reduction in the required variables. A group-coordinate change makes a divisor distinguished in a tame variable. An integral Weierstrass remainder vanishes at high finite characters and hence vanishes identically. This is not a claim about arbitrary fraction-field power series.

Section 4 realizes first-order cup obstructions by moving-prime directions. Joint evaluations and commutators span rectangular or alternating obstruction matrices. Even nullity is needed for the alternating case. A linear combination of realizable directions supplies a full-rank minor. The proof does not claim that one individual Galois element realizes every matrix in the span. Separate tangent slices are preserved when several field tests are treated together. Chebotarev and limits give existence, without a search cost.

### 5. The non-CM single-curve inequality

Integral smoothed Siegel classes supply a Kato class and a period coordinate. Relative modular-symbol lattices, unnormalized traces, smoothing factors and fixed Euler factors are kept explicit. A common multiplier clears torsion and descent ambiguities in any fixed finite switching argument.

The normalized coordinate U is not declared integral merely because its smoothed numerator is integral. The proof treats the vertical divisor, horizontal line switches, and divisors of the smoothing and Euler factors separately. Rectangular classes allow a smoothing factor invertible at each tested horizontal divisor. Bounded numerator and denominator limits then rule out poles, without asserting a uniform denominator for the quotients themselves.

Rank one needs an additional exact logarithmic comparison. A biextension cocycle produces a nonzero tame height direction. A cup-product calculation and a spectral/intersection calculation evaluate the same derivative. Finite differences remove unwanted contributions, and Gross–Zagier supplies the archimedean height comparison. The common trace factor of two cancels on both sides. This identifies the logarithmic ratio up to orientation sign.

The central determinant calculation retains torsion in degrees one and two, the full free point generator, local Haar factors and the period normalization. All omitted Euler factors then cancel, giving the claimed equality X_p(E)=v_p(U(0)) ≥ 0.

### 6. Pair comparison and integral divisibility

The ordinary locus here belongs to the modular source. It does not impose ordinary reduction on the elliptic target. A depleted inverse-derivative limit defines a bounded integral measure, with support on p-adic units. A trace-zero condition fixes the additive constant of the logarithmic primitive.

Finite Fourier summation gives unnormalized CM sums with explicit Gauss factors. Actual analytic series and descended generators of their principal ideals serve different roles. Their central values retain the Manin constant, a single Hilbert-class trace and all local Euler factors.

Analytic primitivity is a separate geometric argument. Local rigidity leads to a relation sheaf of rank h−1 with nonzero projection to all h coordinates. If all generic elliptic factors were nonisogenous, joint monodromy would supply a Frobenius with distinct eigenvalues. Its stable relation subspace would then contain all coordinate axes, a contradiction. Pairwise projection ranks alone do not establish the required full relation-sheaf rank. Class-number cancellation is carried out in the torsion-free fractional ideal group, not by division in Z_p.

At high finite characters, local logarithmic comparisons invert p and allow bounds depending on the fixed character. A monic distinguished polynomial proves the needed flat substitution. Inert-prime square switches preserve the point/class-functional determinant order while dropping fiber dimension by two. Finite-character divisibility lifts through the integral remainder argument. The result is U_w=B_w/L_w in R with p not dividing U_w. Horizontal nonunit factors have not yet been excluded.

### 7–8. Theta vanishing, nonzero content and class extraction

Assuming a horizontal zero of the undepleted analytic series and no jump in either of two primitive strict groups, the argument selects an interior point preserving these conditions and avoiding explicit denominators. Two disjoint auxiliary prime sets avoid a possible fixed-prime exceptional divisor. Character conventions ensure that descent to the norm-one torus does not square the intended character.

The varying-conductor construction needs bounds uniform in position cuts, group coefficients and coefficient extensions. Canonical multiplicative flags add finite etale data and leave the center Fourier parameter unramified. Saturated cusp expansions remove vertical denominators. The moment estimate is coefficientwise in group algebras. Compatible frame changes transform both the cut and polynomial in the same position coordinate. Arbitrary Fourier interchange is not licensed by this argument.

For the local Fourier projection, finite summation cancels the factor p^(-b) exactly before taking a norm. The manuscript explicitly does not bound every inverse Fourier operator uniformly. Binary comparisons cancel matching gamma factors and factorials and cross-multiply identities before specialization. A vanishing Euler polynomial is not inverted at the test point.

Section 8 proves that positive theta content stays nonzero while boundary constants vanish. Two unary periods are treated as analytic functions with separate slot characters. A product-character measure identity, rather than nonvanishing at augmentation alone, controls the prescribed true character. Fixed-stage weights approach zero p-adically while growing as positive integers, before the finite group stage is increased. Uniform boundedness and ultraproduct arguments do not supply an effective convergence rate.

Cusp lifting gives integral operator-order congruences. Inertia or Frobenius separation controls binary projections and removes operator nilpotents with a bounded loss. The resulting four-dimensional Galois representations have limiting blocks f, x and y. A cyclotomic y-to-x extension is eliminated using local strictness and the unit rank of the CM biquadratic field. A highest-weight argument forces a nonzero y-to-f corner. The extracted admissible cocycles are compared back to the finite models using the transferred contractions, in the direction needed to preserve a nonzero class. ### 9. From divisibility to a unit

The theta implication makes every possible quotient factor regular in the tame variables. High-character divisibility in both directions identifies the two quotient ideals, including multiplicities.

New tame directions then force simultaneous strict jumps into codimension at least two. The elementary algebra is made explicit in Lemmas 9.3–9.4. For A alternating of size s and B of size s by 2, the locus rank[A|B] < s has codimension at least two. For fixed nonzero projective v, the equations vᵀA=0 and vᵀB=0 impose s+1 independent conditions, while v varies in dimension s−1. Two independent even alternating matrices also have simultaneous determinant vanishing in codimension two.

A realizable tangent slice transfers this bound to the maximal-minor ideal of a differential over a regular local ring. The kernel inclusion is required over every extension field. Initial forms of the linear minors lie in the initial ideal of the full minors, preserving the dimension bound. The arithmetic work is needed to realize these slices and retain them across all old tests.

An enlarged quotient divisor specializes to a nonzero nonunit old factor and survives as a height-one prime in the relevant localization. Association and the two theta implications force one of the excluded simultaneous jumps there. Thus the enlarged quotients, and then their specializations, are units. Matching nonzero squared factors may be canceled even when they are nonunits at the center.

### 10. Exact central volume and assembly

The all-Kummer determinant has valuation 2 n_P + 2 tau_g − s_K. Both point occurrences contribute the full free index, and torsion in degrees one and three contributes twice tau_g. Passing to strict/full conditions subtracts twice the local logarithmic coordinate and local point torsion. Relaxing the two places over each split non-p prime subtracts twice its local torsion length.

Comparison with the analytic central value gives

\[
2(n_P+\tau_g)=s_K+2v_p(c_E)+2\sum_q v_p(c_q(E)).
\]

The local Haar identities are what replace logarithm and inverse Euler factors by Tamagawa valuations. Substitution into the full Heegner-index identity yields X_p(E)+X_p(E^D)=0.

For CM rank zero, an isogeny over the CM field replaces a nonmaximal endomorphism order by the maximal order. The abelian torsion-extension and nonvanishing hypotheses of Burungale–Flach are then invoked. Restriction of scalars and isogeny invariance make the rational discrepancy have square one. Positivity selects one. Rank-one CM follows from the complementary rank-zero twist. The imported rank converse, two-primary formula and odd-prime conclusions finally assemble the main claim.
