# Full-text reading

A row becomes fully read after personally covering the complete body, proofs, formulas, footnotes, figures, tables, appendices and reference list. Each note first studies the original problem on its own terms. FP relevance is optional and requires a separate, explicit review. Reading a cited result's use in an argument leaves that cited paper's own reading status independent. The [primary index](index.csv) records edition-specific notes in the reading_progress field. A publisher formalization, an independent proof, and a local kernel run each need separate evidence.

All 16 pages of Leike–Hutter's *Bad Universal Priors* were read, including both appendices, tables and references. The dogmatic mixture in Theorem 7 puts its weight on continuing the chosen computable policy while that policy's value is positive. In their finite cutoff Corollary 9, at a history of length equal to the final rewarded round every continuation has value zero, so uniqueness ends earlier. Computability at a single fixed finite horizon permits a finite optimal lookup table. A policy required to compute such optima uniformly in the horizon faces a different problem. The original UAI paper explicitly describes that distinction. [COLT paper, pp. 7–8](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v40/Leike15.pdf#page=7) · [UAI source, p. 8](https://www.hutter1.net/publ/aixicplex.pdf#page=8)

Jiang and Li's complete main paper and all four supplementary pages were read, including all reported simulations and qualifications. Its variance recursion drops a reward/future covariance under an additional conditional-independence assumption. In a two-step single-action MDP let a fair bit be both the stage-one reward and the next state, and let the final reward equal that state. With exact $Q$ and unit discount the doubly robust estimator is twice that bit, so its actual variance is one. The displayed recursion without covariance gives one half. The importance-ratio unbiasedness proof and terminal-only examples still apply. In a three-level binary tree with a uniform logger, a deterministic target path and a terminal Bernoulli payoff of mean $3/4$, exact variances are $3/2$ for the true-$Q$ doubly robust estimator and $87/16$ for stepwise importance sampling. Four sources cited in these two works have their own partial records in the index.
