## Two tools from the first paper in catalog order

In OAI-1006-001, §4 equation (4.2), the defect $t_H(P)-t_N(P)$ is supermodular on isocrystal subobjects. Its values form a finite set and their dimensions are bounded. The largest maximizer is consequently unique and preserved by any symmetry of the data. This reduces a family of tests to invariant subobjects. The proof is elementary once supermodularity is established. The FP coverage objective violates that premise, even after a linear storage price. [Original §4](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/Milnes-rationality-conjecture-for-abelian-varieties-September-23-2026/paper.pdf) · [Explicit two-bit falsifier](research_connections.md#every-optimal-four-bit-synopsis-encodes-higher-order-information)

Lemma 4.2 of the same original chooses a point avoiding *all* nonzero polynomials of degree at most $d$ in $r$ variables with coefficients in a fixed field $K_1$, a finite extension of the specified maximal-unramified coefficient field. It substitutes $X_i=\theta^{N^i}$ with $N>d$ so monomials have distinct exponents, then chooses $\theta$ of degree larger than the resulting maximum exponent. Remark 4.3 identifies why coefficients cannot also vary in the extension containing the witness.

**Direct resource consequence.** More generally, if evaluation at one point in a finite field extension $L/K$ separates every polynomial of total degree at most $d$ over $K$, then $[L:K]\ge\binom{r+d}{d}$. The evaluation map is $K$-linear, and injectivity requires at least the dimension of its domain. If the coefficients range independently over $H+1$ possibilities and a physical output code must distinguish all polynomials, its width satisfies $b\ge\lceil\binom{r+d}{d}\log_2(H+1)\rceil$ by counting. A witness can be one algebraic number while its distinguishable physical states grow rapidly. This is an exact identification claim. Tolerance, restricted families or a noninjective predictive observer can reduce the requirement. Local replay of the manuscript's full arithmetic-geometric argument is pending. ## Field, addition sharing and a real convolution counterexample

Two levels of standard Strassen multiplication on $4\times4$ matrices over the binary field use 49 field products. Flattening the linear forms with separate reductions costs 318 XORs. Executing the recursive schedule costs 198. The published rank-47 algorithm takes 340 XORs with separate reductions, so its two saved field products pay for the extra additions only above respective AND-to-XOR price ratios of 11 and 71, before data movement. Three comparisons use the same field. The DeepMind paper's measured block-matrix acceleration concerns different algorithms and workloads. [Author factors](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphatensor/tree/1949163da3bef7e3eb268a3ac015fd1c2dbfc767/algorithms) · [AlphaTensor](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-022-05172-4)

Supplement C.1's rank-$n$ Fourier argument for cyclic convolution needs roots of unity in the base field. Real length-three convolution is multiplication in $\mathbb R[x]/(x^3-1)\cong\mathbb R\times\mathbb C$. Four real products suffice, using one on the real component and three on the complex component. If three sufficed, the three input linear forms on either side and the three output vectors would each have full rank because multiplication by the unit is invertible. Writing the multiplication slices in these bases gives $M_x=W D_x V$ with $D_x$ diagonal and $D_1$ invertible. Thus $M_xM_1^{-1}=W D_xD_1^{-1}W^{-1}$ would have only real eigenvalues for every $x$. Multiplication by the residue class of the variable is the cyclic shift and has nonreal eigenvalues. The rank is therefore four. [Supplement C.1, p. 10](https://static-content.springer.com/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41586-022-05172-4/MediaObjects/41586_2022_5172_MOESM1_ESM.pdf#page=10)

## Additive moments and unseen task names

Huczynska, Rakhmonov and Yip's Theorems 1.5 and 4.9 maximize additive tuple counts on binary initial segments, with a weighted rearrangement for nonnegative inputs. Under exchangeable unknown parity meanings, these tuple counts are exactly the even moments of the posterior clean-sign bias after normalization. The entropy coefficients are all positive. This gives the exact best configuration at every remaining cardinality, and the complement recursion identifies all minimizers up to affine equivalence. Their Lemma 5.6 gives a separate condition under which locally consistent observed additions extend over unobserved tasks. Opaque names, public task coordinates and confirmed operations have different information content. ## Invariant simplices and the strength of a delayed forecast

Paz's convex-hull realization gives a stochastic update when each input maps every vertex into the hull. For an $L$-dimensional simplex, the chords in a fixed direction with length at least $h$ project to a copy of its original projection scaled by $1-h/H$, where $H$ is the longest chord. This follows by writing the intersection of the simplex and its translate in barycentric coordinates. Applied to the prism forced by a shift of $L$ observable signs, this gives the sharp response $(1-1/L)^{L-1}/L$ for $L+1$ mixture components. The static nesting problem for two squares permits a different scale because it has no input update. The dynamic volume argument has two independent derivations and rational extremizers.
